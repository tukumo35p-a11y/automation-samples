# Tasklane: the simple way for small teams to stay on track

- **See everything at a glance.** One board shows what's in progress, what's blocked, and what's due this week.
- **Set up in 5 minutes.** No training needed. Invite your teammates and start with a ready-made template.
- **Never miss a deadline.** Tasklane reminds the right person before a task is due, not after.
